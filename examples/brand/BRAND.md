# Brand guide (template)

Everything public follows this file. Rules marked *(from taste log)* were promoted from team/creative/taste.md after
the owner approved them.

## Identity
- Channel name / handle: @YourChannel
- One-line promise: what viewers get from you

## Visual
- Palette: background ___, primary ___, accent ___
- Fonts: titles ___, body ___
- Thumbnail formula: e.g. face large on the left, ≤ 4 words, one accent color, nothing in the bottom-right corner
- Never: platform default templates
- *(from taste log)* Repo/LinkedIn art: dark background, bold technical title, the owner's photo

## Voice
- Tone: ___
- Family-friendly policy: ___

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Friendly, like a friend showing you a trick | Salesy or hyped ("insane", "game-changer") |
| Specific: numbers, names, real results | Vague ("tips you need", "level up") |
| Playful with the topic | Mocking the viewer or other creators |

### Kill list
Built from what the owner actually deletes (generic AI-word lists are mostly English and miss your language's
tells). Every row has a reason and a replacement.

| Never | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Ultimate", "game-changer" | Overpromise; our viewers distrust hype | The specific result ("in 3 steps") |
| Opening with "Did you know…" | Every channel in the niche opens like this | Lead with the surprising fact itself |
| "Smash that subscribe button" | Sounds like a demand, not an invitation | One plain invitation at the end |

### Per-surface rules (QA checks these)

| Surface | Max length | Emoji | Required | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube title | 100 UTF-16 units; key phrase in first ~40 chars | 0–1 | The channel's fixed format, if any | ALL CAPS words, kill-list items |
| Short title | ~40 characters | 0–1 | Keyword first | Hashtags in the title |
| Video description | First 2 lines carry the hook | 0–2 | Credits and rights line (original source, owner) | Links not approved by the owner |
| IG caption | Key phrase in first 125 chars; under 2,200 total | 0–2 | One call to action; 3–5 hashtags at the end | "Link in bio" walls, engagement bait |
| Community post | Hook in the first line | 0–1 | One clear ask | Subscribe demands, engagement bait |

## Measurable rules (QA checks these)
- Title ≤ 100 UTF-16 units, key phrase in the first ~40 characters
- Thumbnail text ≤ 4 words, legible at 168 px wide
- Vertical video: text inside x ≤ 860, y ≤ 1480 (1080×1920)
- Loudness −15 LUFS ±1, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP
