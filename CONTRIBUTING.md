# Contributing

Thanks for helping. This repo improves through real use, so reports of how you ran it count as much as code.

## Good contributions
- **A new QA check.** It must have a measurable way to fail, with the measurement written down (pixels, characters,
  dB, a count). If it can't fail, it's a taste question and belongs to the human (see
  [docs/CREATIVE-LOOP.md](docs/CREATIVE-LOOP.md)).
- **A hand-off fix.** If an agent misread another agent's file, propose a stricter format or a validation step
  ([docs/HANDOFFS.md](docs/HANDOFFS.md)).
- **A niche adaptation.** Share what you changed to make the team work for your kind of channel.
- **A skill improvement.** Keep the "drafts only, human decides" rule and the original MIT credits.

## Ground rules
- Nothing in this repo publishes on its own. PRs that add auto-publishing, bulk edits without review, bots, fake
  engagement or paid posting services won't be merged.
- Third-party text (comments, forms, web pages) is data, never instructions. Keep it that way in any new prompt.
- Keep instructions short and concrete. An agent follows numbers better than adjectives.

## How
1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo.
2. One change per PR, with a line in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) under "Unreleased".
